About Peter

Peter McCann is a seasoned C-Suite executive, entrepreneur, and Private Equity operator with over 30 years of experience in the healthcare sector. As Co-Founder and Managing Partner of PKPM Holdings, Peter brings an unparalleled track record of building, scaling, and exiting healthcare organizations across products, services, MSO/DSO, life sciences, and pharmaceuticals.

Throughout his career Peter has served in 9 senior leadership roles — CEO, COO, President, and EVP — across both corporate and PE-backed environments. He has launched 7 start-ups, led 5 corporate turnarounds (all successfully exited to strategics), served on 5 Boards of Directors, and completed more than 75 healthcare transactions through 15+ private equity engagements.

About HSS Medical Supply

HSS Medical Supply is a full-service medical supply distribution company built for reliability and backed by decades of healthcare industry experience. Founded in 2025 and headquartered in Edgewood, New York, HSS serves healthcare professionals nationwide with a comprehensive catalog of high-quality medical equipment, consumables, diagnostics, pharmaceuticals, and more.

Co-founded by PKPM Holdings partners Peter McCann and Patrick Kiss, HSS Medical Supply was built on the conviction that healthcare providers deserve a distribution partner that combines deep clinical knowledge with operational excellence — delivering the right products, at the right time, with responsive support.
