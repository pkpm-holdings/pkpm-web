About Peter

About Peter

Peter McCann is a seasoned C-Suite executive, entrepreneur, and Private Equity operator with over 30 years of experience in the healthcare sector. As Co-Founder and Partner of PKPM Holdings, Peter brings an unparalleled track record of building, scaling, and exiting healthcare organizations across products, services, MSO/DSO, life sciences, and pharmaceuticals.

Throughout his career Peter has served in 9 senior leadership roles — CEO, COO, President, and EVP — across both corporate and PE-backed environments. He has launched 7 start-ups, led 5 corporate turnarounds (all successfully exited to strategics), served on 5 Boards of Directors, and completed more than 75 healthcare transactions through 15+ private equity engagements.
