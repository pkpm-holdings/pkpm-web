About Patrick

About Patrick

Patrick Kiss is a Certified Public Accountant and Co-Founder of PKPM Holdings, bringing a rare combination of Big 4 audit experience, healthcare finance expertise, and hands-on M&A execution to every client engagement. Patrick co-founded PKPM Holdings with the mission of providing excellent business consulting services and building a full-service medical supply distribution business.

With a foundation built at PwC and honed through progressive leadership at Growth Orthopedics — a Trivest Partners-backed orthopedic MSO — Patrick brings the financial rigor of institutional consulting to healthcare organizations of all sizes.
