About Calvin

Calvin Adam is a senior at Baruch College majoring in Accounting with a minor in Communications. As an Associate at PKPM Holdings, Calvin brings a sharp analytical foundation and a forward-thinking approach to healthcare operations and advisory work.

At Frankel Loughran Starr & Vallone, Calvin supports the firm's private client services practice, working on tax compliance and planning engagements for high-net-worth individuals, families, and closely held entities.

At HSS Medical Supply, Calvin leads efforts to improve operational efficiency by deploying AI-driven tools across internal and external processes. He also manages GPO contract relationships, working to increase gross margin and deliver better pricing outcomes for customers.
