Pete McCann Joins Ally Technical Board After FFL Partners Investment

Althea US Inc. has rebranded as Ally Technical alongside a new investment from FFL Partners. PKPM Holdings Partner Pete McCann assisted in the transaction and now serves on the company’s Board of Directors.

Althea US Inc., a provider of medical device service, repair, and parts, has rebranded as Ally Technical. The rebrand comes alongside a new investment from FFL Partners, a private equity firm that partners with healthcare, business services, and technology companies. With the new investment, Ally Technical is expanding its medical device service, repair, and parts business.

Pete McCann, Partner and Co-Founder of PKPM Holdings, assisted in the transaction and has joined the Ally Technical Board of Directors.

Experience behind the board seat

Pete brings more than 30 years of healthcare experience to the role, including over 75 healthcare transactions completed through more than 15 private equity engagements, and prior service on five boards of directors. His work at PKPM Holdings includes advising private equity and venture capital investors and launching healthcare businesses in areas including healthcare technology management (HTM) and distribution.

FFL Partners is one of the private equity firms PKPM Holdings works with across healthcare and private capital.
