HSS Medical Supply Featured in Repertoire Magazine

Repertoire Magazine profiled HSS Medical Supply, the medical supply distributor co-founded by PKPM Holdings partners Pete McCann and Patrick Kiss, in a feature titled “A Start-Up with Old-School Values.”

For Pete McCann and Patrick Kiss, the spark that ignited HSS Medical Supply arrived on “a simple phone call on a random November afternoon.” Repertoire Magazine covered the launch story, the founding culture, and the vision behind the company.

About HSS Medical Supply

HSS Medical Supply is a full-service medical supply distribution company headquartered in Edgewood, New York. It serves healthcare professionals nationwide with medical equipment, consumables, lab and diagnostics, pharmaceuticals, and more, across categories including med-surg, wound care, infection control, respiratory, and surgical instruments.

HSS is a PKPM Holdings portfolio company. Pete McCann serves as President and Patrick Kiss, CPA as Vice President.
