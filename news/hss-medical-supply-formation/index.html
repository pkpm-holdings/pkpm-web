PKPM Holdings Announces the Formation of HSS Medical Supply

PKPM Holdings has announced the formation of HSS Medical Supply, a full-service medical supply distribution company co-founded by Peter McCann and Patrick Kiss, CPA. The launch was covered in Long Island Business News.

“The HSS Promise is simple: Our success begins with our customers, our team, and the patients we all serve.”Patrick Kiss, CPA, Co-Founder

HSS Medical Supply was built on the conviction that healthcare providers deserve a distribution partner that combines deep clinical knowledge with operational excellence: delivering the right products, at the right time, with responsive support.

What HSS offers

Headquartered in Edgewood, New York, HSS serves healthcare professionals nationwide with a catalog of more than 1,000 products, including lab and diagnostics, med-surg supplies, dental, medical equipment, pharmaceuticals, OTC products, wound care, infection control, rehab and PT, respiratory, home healthcare, and surgical instruments, with 24/7 online ordering.

Leadership

Peter McCann, President and Co-Founder, is a 30-year healthcare veteran and private equity operator. Patrick Kiss, CPA, Vice President and Co-Founder, oversees financial operations, vendor relationships, and operational infrastructure.
